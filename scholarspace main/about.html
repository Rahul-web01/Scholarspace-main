I’m a backend developer who enjoys building APIs and working with data. I built ScholarSpace because study material at university is scattered across WhatsApp groups and private drives. ScholarSpace puts it in one place, organised by year and subject, free for everyone.

Mission

Make good study material free and easy to find for every student, in every course.

How it’s organised

B.Tech CSE follows the BBD University 2026-27 syllabus year by year. Other courses are grouped by subject.

Built with students

Notes come from students, toppers and teachers. Anyone can contribute.

What ScholarSpace offers

  • One searchable library of notes, previous year papers, practice sets and books.
  • Year-wise subjects for B.Tech CSE, matched to the current syllabus.
  • Material for other branches, B.Pharm and Agriculture.
  • An SGPA calculator on the 10-point grading scale.
