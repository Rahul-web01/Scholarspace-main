About ScholarSpace

Mission

Make good study material free and easy to find for every student, in every course.

How it’s organised

B.Tech CSE is split by college: BBD University (2026-27 syllabus) and ITM College (AKTU syllabus), each arranged year by year, because their notes and exam difficulty differ. BBA follows the BBD University 2023-24 (NEP 2020) syllabus year by year. Other courses are grouped by subject.

Built with students

Anyone can upload notes or question papers. Every upload is checked before it appears on the site.
