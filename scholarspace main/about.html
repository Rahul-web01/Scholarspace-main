Why it exists

Before every exam, the same search begins: notes buried in WhatsApp groups, papers on someone’s phone, a senior’s Drive link that no longer works. Students waste hours finding material instead of studying it.

ScholarSpace puts it all in one place. Every subject follows the university syllabus, every file opens in one click, and nothing needs a login or a payment.

What you’ll find

  • B.Tech CSE for BBD University and ITM College (AKTU), kept separate because their syllabi differ
  • BBA arranged by the BBD University 2023-24 syllabus
  • Other branches, B.Pharm, Agriculture and official syllabus PDFs
  • Search across every subject and file, and an SGPA calculator

How it works

1. Find your subject

Pick your course and year, or search for a subject, unit or topic.

2. Study for free

View or download notes, previous year papers and practice sets. No sign-up needed.

3. Contribute

Have notes or papers? Share them from the Contribute page. Every file is reviewed before it goes live.
